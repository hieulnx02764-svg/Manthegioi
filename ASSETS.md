Phong cách chung: premium miniature pixel art; 32–48 logical pixel silhouettes; large deliberate pixel clusters; warm storybook palette; dark teal outlines; three tones per material; upper-left light; readable at tiny 16 px; true transparent background; no matte, text, gridlines, texture noise or colored fringe; centered sprites with 14% transparent margin. Humans face right; sheep and wolf face left. Preserve exact equal cell layout and subject order.

1. **sprites 4×4:** broadleaf tree, conifer, mountain, berry shrub; blue human, red human, white sheep, gray wolf; straw cottage, red cottage, castle, wheat; flame, meteor, boat, gold ore.
2. **civil-sprites 2×2:** blonde green-cloaked elf, stout bearded dwarf; green orc, golden crystal shrine.
3. **economy-sprites 4×4:** blue goblin, orange fox beastkin, iron, coal; copper, gold, purple crystal, stone; mine, forge, warehouse, market; watchtower, well, fishing hut, merchant cart.
4. **ancient-sprites 4×2, tạo mới:** Stegosaurus with orange dorsal plates, brown upright Allosaurus, blue-gray Triceratops with three ivory horns, olive raptor; shaggy mammoth with curled ivory tusks, golden saber-toothed cat, ancient fern/palm, cactus. All creatures face right and have feet on a shared cell baseline. Same palette, lighting, outlines, transparency and margins as above; cute natural proportions and recognizable species; overhead 3/4 perspective.
